Your diary. Your device. Nobody else's.

Dearest is a simple, open-source diary and journaling app built to be fully private and fully offline. Every entry is encrypted on your device with a passphrase only you know. There is no account and no server, and no way for anyone, including the developer, to read your journal.

<b>Privacy</b>
• Encrypted at rest: your whole journal is encrypted with AES-256 (SQLCipher).
• No recovery, by design: there is no password reset and no backdoor. Your passphrase is the only key, so if you forget it your entries cannot be recovered.
• Fully offline: Dearest requests no internet permission at all, so it cannot send your data anywhere. You can check this in your system's app settings.
• Optional biometric unlock as a convenience; your passphrase always works as a fallback.
• Auto-lock with a timeout you choose, a privacy screen in the app switcher, screenshots and screen recording blocked, and an escalating delay after repeated wrong passphrases.
• Nothing is included in cloud backups or device-to-device transfers.

<b>Writing</b>
• Notebooks to separate personal, work, dreams and anything else.
• Tags that cut across notebooks.
• Full-text search, filtering by notebook and tag, and sorting by newest, oldest or title.
• Calendar view showing the days you wrote on.
• Drafts are saved automatically as you type.
• Optional daily reminder.

<b>Your data, your way</b>
• Create an encrypted backup protected by a password you choose, including notebooks, entries and tags.
• Or export plain text, CSV or JSON if you want a portable, readable copy.

<b>Appearance</b>
• System, light or dark theme, higher contrast options, and Material You dynamic color.

<b>Crash reports</b>
If the app hits an unexpected error, you can copy or save the technical details on the next launch. Nothing from your journal is included and nothing is sent automatically.
